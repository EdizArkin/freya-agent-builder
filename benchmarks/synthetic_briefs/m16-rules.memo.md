# Memo M: four amendments to Brief A and Brief B

Synthetic. Written for this repository to carry four kinds of rule from a document to a deployed agent. It is not hackathon material and belongs to no evaluation package. It is given after Brief A and Brief B and amends them: where it differs from a brief, this memo wins. Everything it does not mention stays as the briefs have it.

## What changes

M1. Amounts in steps. A promise is recorded only for an amount that is a whole multiple of 20.00. The full past-due amount of a product is always accepted, also when it is not such a multiple. The floor and the full amount of the product still apply. If the customer names an amount that is not such a multiple, say that amounts are recorded in steps of 20 and offer the nearest amounts that can be recorded.

M2. An early date needs half. A promise dated today or on the next business day is recorded only if its amount is at least half of the past-due amount of that product. A lower amount, down to the floor, needs a date after the next business day. If the customer offers a lower amount for today or for the next business day, say so and give the choice: at least half by the next business day, or the amount they named on a later date.

M3. One date for all products. When a customer has several late products, every promise recorded in one call carries the same date. This replaces rule 6 of Brief B: no product is recorded on its own deadline while another product keeps a different date. Before the first promise is recorded, agree one date that is not after the deadline of any product that gets a promise, and say in the read-back that this one date applies to all of them.

M4. Break of the callback team. On every day no callback is booked from 12:00 up to, but not including, 13:00. A callback at 13:00 is possible. A time inside the break counts as a time outside the window for rule 7 of Brief A: ask once for another time.
